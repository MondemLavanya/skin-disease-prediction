Machine Learning Implication of Class Imbalance:

Because Melanocytic Nevi (nv) accounts for ~67% of samples, a standard model may easily develop a majority-class bias. In our pipeline, we address this during training using:

  • Data Augmentation (flips, rotations, contrast adjustments) on minority classes.
  • Class-Weighted Loss Functions (penalizing misclassification of Melanoma more heavily).
  • Stratified Dataset Splitting to ensure proportional representation across splits.
