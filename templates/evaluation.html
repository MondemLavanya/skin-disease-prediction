The "Accuracy Paradox" in Medical Imaging

Why raw accuracy alone is insufficient for project evaluation

In the benchmark HAM10000 dataset, Melanocytic Nevi (nv) represents 67% of all samples. Consider a trivial, completely un-trained model that simply predicts "Melanocytic Nevi" for every single image:

67.0% Apparent "High" Accuracy
0.0% Melanoma Sensitivity (Fatal)
0.0% Basal Cell Carcinoma Recall

Such a model would pass a superficial accuracy threshold while missing 100% of life-threatening malignancies. Consequently, medical AI projects are evaluated primarily on Macro Sensitivity (Recall), Specificity, and Confusion Matrices rather than accuracy alone.
